- Prompt inicial: Use case: logo-brand. Asset: a finished compact emblem for the student technology hackathon team QuantumByte, used on the CampusTwin AI dashboard. Design a distinctive abstract letter Q made of two clean interlocking circuit-like ribbon segments, with one small square byte at the lower-right terminal. Precise friendly geometric silhouette, elegant rounded corners, bold broad strokes, very readable at 32px and 48px. Teal emerald and luminous mint with a restrained blue accent, compatible with a deep navy #122033 sidebar and a white header. Flat vector-like graphic, extremely subtle internal gradient allowed, no 3D extrusion, no shadow outside the mark, no glow. One isolated centered emblem with tight balanced margins, occupies 85% of square canvas. Genuinely transparent background. No text, no letters other than the designed Q monogram, no slogan, no mockup, no presentation board. Do not include any university crest.
- Prompt de refinamiento final: Use case: logo-brand, precise refinement of the attached QuantumByte Q emblem for a real website. Preserve the readable Q silhouette, the two circuit terminals and the small blue square terminal. Clean and simplify it into a professional crisp FLAT geometric mark using three solid fills only: mint #54e8ba, deep teal #087d74, blue #218bdf. Remove every stray pixel, speckle, noise, fuzzy fringe, particle, texture and cast shadow, especially inside the Q counter and around its lower-right tail. Perfectly clean continuous anti-aliased boundaries, no glow, no gradients, no soft shading. One centered isolated Q, tight 8% margins. Absolutely no text or other elements. Preserve true transparent empty background.

## Independencia institucional

CampusTwin AI es un prototipo independiente de QuantumByte. El escudo institucional ya no se usa en la interfaz. El catálogo es ficticio, las reservas son de demostración y la telemetría del sensor es experimental; las grabaciones se identifican como muestras registradas.

Se retiró del repositorio `san-marcos-official.svg` tras comprobar que no tiene referencias activas ni dependencias. Al eliminarlo de `public/brand`, el build deja de distribuir el recurso institucional. Se conserva el logo propio `quantumbyte-mark-v1.png`.
